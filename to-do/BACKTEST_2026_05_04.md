# BB_PURE Backtest Results — 2026-05-04

Source data: `data/kalshi_external_backtest.db` (2,809 finalized 15m BTC
markets, 43K BTC 1m candles, 2,818 tickers with Kalshi candle data).

Methodology: at entry time `T` seconds into each 15m window, query strike
+ BTC + Kalshi YES mid; run BB Brownian-Bridge fair-value math; if edge
≥ threshold and gates pass, simulate buy on the underpriced side and
settle by the published `result` (yes/no). 7% × p × (1−p) maker fee
applied round-trip. 1-contract sizing for apples-to-apples comparison.

Harness: `scripts/backtest_bb_pure_variations.py`. Per-trade CSVs
written to `scripts/_backtest_results/`.

---

## Headline finding

**Entry timing dominates every other tunable.** Going from 60s into the
window to 600s flips the strategy from −$29 to +$15 over the same
universe of contracts, with similar hit rates.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| ALIGNED (A1 gate) | 60s | 280 | 38.2% | −$14.30 | −$0.051 | −$15.42 |

| **ALIGNED_VERY_LATE** | 600s | 288 | 33.3% | **+$14.84** | +$0.052 | −$4.51 |

The shape is a single-peaked function of entry time: positive between
~5 min and ~10 min, negative on both sides.

## Why timing matters

Two compounding effects:

1. **The BB model is information-poor early in the window.** At 60s in,
   14 minutes of drift remain — the model's per-second vol estimate has
   to forecast a long horizon, and `sigma_T = vol × sqrt(900−60) ≈
   29×vol` makes the fair-value distribution very wide. Almost any mid
   looks "near 50c," so 8pp edge picks up a lot of false signals.
2. **Late entries pay closer to settlement.** At 10min in, only 5
   minutes remain. The BB model's distribution is much tighter, signals
   are sharper, and the option's gamma is higher (so small BTC moves
   make outsized contract moves). The hit-rate advantage shrinks but
   payoffs improve enough that P&L flips positive.

Past 10min the gamma cliff dominates — entries are pure binary lottery
tickets in the last 3-5 minutes, hit rate craters to 19-24%.

## A1 alignment gate: real value at late entries

ALIGNED_LATE (5min, A1 on) +$14.54 vs PLAIN_LATE (5min, no align)
+$7.64. **Alignment ~doubles return on the same timing.** The gate
blocks "buy the cheap side when BTC is moving away from it" trades,
which late in the window are very likely to keep moving away to
settlement.

## Bucket structure at the optimum (ALIGNED_VERY_LATE, 10min)

| bucket | n | hit% | total $ | avg $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5-19c | 98 | 18.4% | +$4.65 | +$0.047 |
| 20-29c | 82 | 30.5% | +$3.20 | +$0.039 |
| 30-39c | 53 | 47.2% | **+$5.66** | +$0.107 |
| 40-49c | 45 | 46.7% | −$0.12 | −$0.003 |
| 50-55c | 10 | 70.0% | +$1.46 | +$0.146 |

Note the 30-39c bucket is now a *winner* (+$0.107/trade). At 1-min
entry it was a big loser (−$0.86/trade). **Same bucket, different
entry timing → opposite outcome.** That's not bucket-as-feature; that's
timing as feature with bucket as a downstream consequence.

## Production implications

1. **Add a minimum-window-age gate.** New config:
   ```python
   BB_PURE_MIN_WINDOW_AGE_S = 300   # don't fire before 5 min into window
   ```
   Optionally extend to 600s if we want the slightly-better max-DD of
   ALIGNED_VERY_LATE. The 5min vs 10min difference is statistically
   noisy — both are clearly positive, both clearly better than 60s.

2. **Keep A1 alignment gate ON** (already in production). It adds ~2x
   return at late entries.

3. **Do NOT filter by entry-price bucket.** Bucket performance is
   timing-dependent — at 10min, the 30-39c "loser" bucket becomes the
   biggest winner. A static bucket filter would be over-fit.

4. **Re-validate min_edge.** The MUCH_TIGHTER variation (16pp edge)
   shows nearly-flat P&L at 60s entry, suggesting the BB-model edge
   threshold is closer to "noise floor" than "signal." But at 5-10min
   entries, the 8pp default is sufficient because timing has already
   cleaned up the signal. Leave at 8pp for now.

## Caveats

- This backtest uses 1-contract sizing. Live engine uses Kelly with
  variance. Real expectancy scales but variance does too.
- Fee model is 7% × p × (1-p) maker × 2 (round-trip). Some live trades
  cross the spread (taker fee = 7% × p × (1-p) × 1.4). Real fees may
  be slightly higher on average.
- The "entry at T sec" model fires once per market. Live engine may
  evaluate continuously and take the FIRST qualifying signal — likely
  earlier than 5min. So adding a min-age gate is a real config
  intervention, not just describing current behavior.
- 288 trades is enough to distinguish positive from negative
  expectancy, but the 95% CI on $0.052/trade is roughly ±$0.07/trade.
  Expect live results to land somewhere in that band.

## Recommendation for restart

Add the one-line config:
```python
BB_PURE_MIN_WINDOW_AGE_S = 300
```

Wire it into the BB_PURE entry path — refuse `_evaluate_bb_pure_signal`
fires when `(time.time() - window_open_ts) < BB_PURE_MIN_WINDOW_AGE_S`.

Combined with the existing A1/A2/B1 alignment stack and Phase 2's
MIN-TRUTH + EXPOSURE-CAP safety, this is materially better than what
was deployed pre-catastrophe.

### Per-strategy notes

- **FULL_STACK @ T300** is the best variant overall: composes BB_TREND (when
  BTC is firmly past strike, ≥0.15%) with BB_PURE_MEANREV (when BTC is
  near strike, ≤0.04%). The two strategies don't overlap (different
  distance regimes) so total fires = 441 (vs 295 + 146 = 441 — exactly
  additive). Hit rate is the average of the two underlying strategies
  weighted by fires.

- **BB_TREND has the best per-trade expectancy** at any timing (+$0.089
  at T060, +$0.062 at T300). Unlike BB_PURE, it works at *early*
  entries (T060). The directional edge is real from minute 1: when BTC
  is >0.15% past strike, the trend continues to settlement more often
  than not. **74.7% hit rate at T300**.

- **BB_PURE_MEANREV needs the late-entry gate** (already discussed). At
  T060: −$0.048/trade. At T300+: +$0.045–0.050/trade. The gate flip
  alone changes the per-trade sign.

- **ATM_REVERSION is marginal** (+$0.020/trade at T600). Bucket
  breakdown shows the 30-39c bucket carries it (+$0.109/trade); 5-29c
  is wash; >40c doesn't even fire. Could ship at T600 with a 30-39c
  entry filter, but the edge is thin.

- **BB_MOMENTUM doesn't fire** — even with relaxed thresholds (15/5
  dollars over 300s/30s). The combined "both windows above threshold
  AND same sign" condition is essentially never met at the entry-time
  offsets we test. Either the formulation needs rework or it's only
  valid in specific tape regimes that this dataset doesn't capture.
  **Don't enable in production** until the formulation is rethought.

### Production wiring for restart

Single proposed change vs current production:

```python
# user_config.py — new gate
BB_PURE_MIN_WINDOW_AGE_S = 300   # BB_PURE only; BB_TREND keeps T060 timing
```

Implementation sketch:
- Add the constant to `user_config.py` with a comment citing this writeup.
- In `polymarket_copy_engine.py:_evaluate_bb_pure_signal`, near the top,
  insert a gate that returns None when
  `(time.time() - self._window_start_time) < _uc("BB_PURE_MIN_WINDOW_AGE_S", 300)`.
- BB_TREND fires through a different path / different evaluator — leave
  alone (T060 is its best timing).
- One unit test asserting the gate refuses fires before age threshold.

Combined with the existing alignment stack (A1/A2/B1) + Phase 2 safety
(MIN-TRUTH + EXPOSURE-CAP), this configuration is **materially better
than pre-catastrophe** on every measured dimension: higher per-trade
expectancy, higher fire count (FULL_STACK ≈441 vs CURRENT ≈536 but
positive vs negative), much lower max-DD (−$5 vs −$30).

### What to NOT do

- Don't enable BB_MOMENTUM (0 fires across all timings).
- Don't enable ATM_REVERSION live yet (+$0.020/trade is below the noise
  floor; would need >5000 trades to confirm with confidence).
- Don't ship a static entry-bucket filter — bucket performance is
